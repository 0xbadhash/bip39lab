## Problem Statement

After UC14 shipped as a thin dice pad, learners could not see running bits, 24-word / 256-bit need, Lab stills (dice / key / lock), or pad+passphrase as a stack. UC11 you-hold mixed one-signer and 2-of-3 in one column; 0.184 bitcoin was easy to miss.

## Solution

UC14: live ~N bits face, 12–24 generate from the pad, sufficient indicator vs 128/256, Lab dice + lock stills (lock hue-rotate red→blue→green). UC15: same pad + passphrase estimate stack + key still. UC11: huge 0.184 bitcoin; you-hold two columns (one signer | co-signer).

## User Stories

1. As a learner, I see ~N bits grow as I roll, including past 256 (e.g. ~317).
2. As a learner, I pick 12–24 words, mint from the pad, and the indicator stays TOO LOW until pad bits meet that length.
3. As a learner, dice / key / lock stills match Lab chapter art; lock tints with entropy.
4. As a learner, UC15 shows pad + phrase need + passphrase estimate; a long PP does not fix a short pad.
5. As a learner, UC11 shows **0.184 bitcoin** large; freeze keeps the number; you-hold is two columns.
