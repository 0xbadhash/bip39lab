## Problem Statement

M=1 is singlesig with extra paperwork. `#msPolicy` does not say so.

## Solution

If M=1 after build, `#msPolicy` (or `#msM1Warn`) warns 1-of-N is not stronger than singlesig. Always show lose-1-key / compromise-1-vendor / lose-map-and-1-key preview lines.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] M=1 build shows the warning
- [ ] M≥2 build does not show M=1 warning
- [ ] Policy preview three failure lines present after any successful build
- [ ] Playwright **S76**

## Out of Scope

Blocking M=1 in the calculator (still allowed for teaching).
