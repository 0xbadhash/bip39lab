## Problem Statement

UC1 concept chips were text-only so the three App Shell atoms were unused. The rail stacked under “Track n/6” stole height so SVGs were capped tiny. Generate buried BIP-39 (i) above the button. Addresses stacked `#n` over `tb1q`. UC2 still used grey chips and a quiz that nags “Select both right sentences (2 and 3).”

## Solution

Mount UC1 and UC2 atoms in the concept strip (`uc1SetViz` / `uc2SetViz`). Slim inline rail. Generate toolbar: mnemonic (i) beside Generate, Clear secrets right. Address pairs: chip beside rounded `tb1q`, three per row. UC2 quiz: wrong explains why; partial correct is silent.

## User Stories

1. As a learner on UC1, I see Plan / Practice / Review atoms change with the rail.
2. As a learner on Validate, I read `#0 tb1q…` inline, three pairs on a row.
3. As a learner on UC2, I pick one right quiz sentence without being told to pick 2 and 3.
