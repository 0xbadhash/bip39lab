## Problem Statement

Compare still had a Compare button after live updates. Passphrase strength was plain text. On the pad card, **Build N practice words from this pad** sat on the far right of a `space-between` gen bar, away from **Word count**.

## Solution

Drop Compare; unlock Next when addresses diverge. Color estimate tiers (empty/weak/fair/stronger). Group word-count select and Build button in `.v2-ent-mint-group` with `flex-start`. Dual stamp: product `0.16.53` vs chip `0.17.80-v2`. Classic `/` unchanged except VERSION stamp.
