What is Shamir sharing?

Shamir splits one secret into N shares so any M shares rebuild it — with a correct implementation, fewer than M reveal nothing useful. That is different from Multisig (M-of-N keys that co-sign a spend) and from a BIP-39 phrase (one recovery word list — not a threshold split).

When would you use this? Shamir is for backup / inheritance of one secret (split custody of the phrase itself). Multisig is for ongoing spend policy (several keys must sign). This page is a math demo only — format is not SLIP-39 and is not audited for real funds; the “not for real funds” warning is about compatibility and hardening, not “the math is fake.”
