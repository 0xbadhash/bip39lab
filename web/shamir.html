What is Shamir sharing?

Split one secret into N pieces so any M pieces rebuild it — and fewer than M reveal nothing useful.

Shamir (this page) Multisig BIP-39 phrase
What is split? One secret (bytes) N separate keys Not split — one word list
M-of-N means M shares rebuild the secret M signatures authorize a spend N/A
This lab format share:index:hex (edu) Addresses from pubkeys English wordlist

Threshold secret sharing is not the same as a multisig vault address.
