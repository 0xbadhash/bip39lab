What this page is

Like checking bank fees and account balances online — without logging into your vault keys. Nothing here needs your recovery phrase. If you look up an address, that service learns you care about it (like typing an account number into a public search box).

Is: fee snapshot, how busy the mempool looks, optional balance for addresses you paste or brought from Lab.
Is not: a wallet, price charts, or spending tools. Failures show as unknown/error — never a fake “0” when the API fails.

Fees & traffic

Fee rate (sat/vB) is like a postage stamp price for Bitcoin transactions — higher often confirms faster when the network is busy. Example cost assumes ~140 vB (simple modern send) — an estimate only.
